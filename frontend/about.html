Building technology that listens. Rama Krishna is the developer and visionary behind MindCursor — a platform dedicated to autism awareness, compassionate AI, and the belief that every mind maps the world differently.

From curiosity
to compassion

Rama Krishna is a software engineer and human-computer interaction researcher passionate about making mental-health tools more accessible, honest, and kind. With a deep interest in neurodiversity, Rama set out to bridge the gap between clinical knowledge and everyday understanding.

The seed for MindCursor was planted after observing how families struggle to find clear, stigma-free information about autism spectrum traits. Most tools were either overly clinical or dismissive. Rama imagined something different — a platform that felt like a thoughtful conversation, not a diagnosis.

With a background spanning full-stack development, UX design, and applied AI, Rama combined those disciplines into a single cohesive product. Every interaction on MindCursor — from the animated brain on the homepage to the step-by-step screening flow — was crafted with the same intention: meet people where they are, without judgment.

Outside of code, Rama is an advocate for inclusive education, a reader of cognitive science literature, and a firm believer that technology's greatest superpower is empathy.
